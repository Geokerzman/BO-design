# Design brief — Datenschutz page

Build the German privacy policy page (`Datenschutz`). This is a legally required page, not a marketing page. Its entire job is to be readable and easy to navigate.

Once it exists, repoint the `Datenschutzerklärung` link in the contact form consent checkbox from `#` to this page. Also repoint the `Datenschutz` link in the site footer.

---

## Design

Identical to the Impressum page. Same quiet treatment:

- `#FFFFFF` background, `#111111` text, `#6B7280` secondary, `#E5E7EB` hairlines.
- `#3B82F6` used only for links and the visible keyboard focus state. Nowhere else.
- Same geometric grotesque family. Page title Bold; section headings Bold at body size, not display size. Body 16px minimum, leading 1.7.
- Same sticky nav: `BO Design` left, `Get a quote` button right.
- Single centred column, max width 640px, generous top and bottom padding. 24px side padding on mobile.
- Sections separated by a hairline rule with roughly 48px above and below.
- No cards, no icons, no images, no motion, no scroll animations.

**One addition this page needs:** a table of contents directly under the page title — a plain hairline-separated list of the twelve section headings, each an anchor link in `#3B82F6`. No numbering styling, no box around it. The page is long and people arrive looking for one specific section.

Numbered headings stay exactly as written in the content below — the numbering is part of the legal text, not a design decision.

## Constraints

- Email as `mailto:`, phone as `tel:`.
- Text selectable and copyable.
- Semantic heading order; anchor targets must work with keyboard navigation.
- Leave the square-bracket placeholders visible so they can be filled in before launch.
